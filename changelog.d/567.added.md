Added a repository-side PR title guard (`.github/workflows/pr-title-lint.yml` backed by `tools/check_pr_title.py`) that rejects agent-branded PR title prefixes such as `[codex]`, `[claude]`, `[openai]`, and `[chatgpt]`, and enforces the conventional `<type>(<optional-scope>): <subject>` shape with a lowercase-leading subject on every target branch including `dev`. The same validator powers local tests so the policy cannot silently drift from the workflow.
