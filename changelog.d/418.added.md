`Node.runtime.service_manager_units` records observed service-manager (systemd) unit state — `load_state`, `active_state`, `sub_state`, `enabled_state`, `result`, optional `main_pid`, `unit_file_path`, redactable `exec_start`, and same-node `Node.services[]` refs — distinct from transport services, live processes, packages, content, and authored conditions. See ADR-035.
