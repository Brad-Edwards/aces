Added the realization-envelope relation: a versioned, closed envelope expression
contract (`aces_contracts.realization_envelope`) plus a deterministic membership,
subsumption, witness-generation, and negative-probe relation over SDL scenario
instances (`aces_sdl.realization_envelope`), implementing ADR-070 /
`specs/formal/realization/envelope-semantics.md`. Authors and backends can now
express a family of scenario instances and decide, without a solver or a
hard-coded reference scenario, whether a concrete scenario is in the family,
whether one family is contained in another, derive a validated in-family witness,
and generate out-of-family probes for closed envelopes.
