### Added

- Published SEM-224 observability plane separation semantics: a carrier-oriented plane classifier (`aces_sdl.observability_plane_semantics`) that assigns each claim-bearing observability/evidence artifact exactly one of the five named planes — scenario-native observability, authored evidence requirement, processor/backend operational observability, captured evidence, and derived analysis — by carrier role rather than by free-form strings such as `log`, `trace`, or `evidence`. The three claim-bearing experiment-core contracts (`experiment-capture-spec-v1`, `experiment-evidence-record-v1`, `experiment-derived-measure-v1`) now publish their plane as a portable `x-aces-plane` schema annotation sourced from that classifier.
