Added `examples/scenarios/cage-2-scenario2.sdl.yaml`, the TTCP CAGE Challenge 2
(Scenario2) scenario authored as a single ACES SDL document (REP-003, ADR-069):
the three-subnet topology (user / enterprise / critical operational-OT), hosts
and services, red/blue/green/evaluator identities, the red foothold and pivot
path to the critical Op_Server0 OT service, and the defensive scoring pipeline
and objectives. It resolves, verifies imports, and publishes cleanly through the
reference implementation, and is the single authored source of truth both
backends realize in the CAGE-2 replication program. A companion
`cage-2-scenario2.README.md` records the upstream source pins, the CAGE-to-ACES
name mapping, and loss disclosures.
