# ASR-508 — Participant Validation Scenario Corpus

## Statement

The ecosystem shall maintain validation scenarios covering offensive, defensive, baseline, autonomous-service, targetable-participant, and mixed-control participant behavior.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Participant behavior needs representative scenario coverage across the foreseeable role space.
