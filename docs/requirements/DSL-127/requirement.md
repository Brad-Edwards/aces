# DSL-127 — Time Progression And Synchronization Surface

## Statement

The language shall support authored constraints or declarations for pacing, stepping, advancement, synchronization, dilation, and comparable time-progression concerns where relevant.

## Rationale

Cross-domain simulation and emulation literature shows that time advancement and synchronization policy materially affect system behavior and cannot always be left implicit.
