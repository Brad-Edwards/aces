# ASR-522 — Trajectory Split, Source, And Lineage Integrity

## Statement

The ecosystem shall provide assurance mechanisms that verify declared trajectory-corpus split semantics, source semantics, and lineage integrity where such claims are made.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that training and evaluation claims depend on trustworthy split, source, and lineage handling for participant corpora.
