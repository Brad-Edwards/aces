# AUT-802 — RAES Human Command-Line Semantic Surface

## Statement

RAES shall provide a human-facing command-line surface for parsing, validating, normalizing, resolving, compiling, transforming, and inspecting RAES SDL and portable contracts, and for invoking RAES conformance checks. Pack layout, publication, and pack-aware workflows belong to env-packs; realization, lifecycle, and experiment execution belong to conformant backends.

## Rationale

Narrowed after establishment of RAES Hub, env-packs, adapters, and LilRAE ownership boundaries. RAES retains only CLI behavior over its semantic and conformance authority.
