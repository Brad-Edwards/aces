# ASR-528 — Time Semantics And Clock Disclosure Conformance

## Statement

The ecosystem shall provide conformance mechanisms that verify temporal semantics, clock and time-domain declarations, ordering guarantees, and run-level time-model disclosure claims.

## Rationale

Once time is a first-class portability concern, assurance has to check both semantic consistency and honest disclosure of realized time behavior.
