# ACT-613 — Targetable Participants And Services

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support participants and autonomous services that can be modeled both as actors and as targetable scenario elements.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Scenarios need to represent participants that can both act and be acted upon.
