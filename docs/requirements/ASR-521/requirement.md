# ASR-521 — Participant Benchmark Protocol Conformance

## Statement

The ecosystem shall maintain conformance fixtures and checks for participant-oriented benchmark protocols, verifier outputs, judge outputs, and reward contracts.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that benchmark and verifier protocol claims need executable conformance evidence, not just prose descriptions.
