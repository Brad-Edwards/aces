# GOV-915 — Corpus, Benchmark, And Verifier Versioning

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support explicit versioning and compatibility tracking for participant task corpora, trajectory corpora, benchmark protocols, and verifier or judge assets.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that participant-supporting ecosystems depend on explicit versioning of datasets, benchmarks, and verifier assets to make comparisons and claims meaningful.
