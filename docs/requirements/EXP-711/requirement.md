# EXP-711 — Cross-Run Comparability

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support explicit comparability semantics across runs, including what constitutes a comparable task, setup, evidence basis, and derived measure set.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Comparative analysis needs explicit comparability semantics rather than ad hoc assumptions.
