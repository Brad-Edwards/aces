# EXP-717 — Evidence Integrity And Chain Of Custody

## Statement

The ecosystem shall preserve integrity and chain-of-custody information for captured evidence and derived outputs where experiment claims depend on them.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Evidence capture is not sufficient on its own when experiment conclusions depend on trustworthy custody and transformation history.
