# GOV-914 — Experiment Safety And Guardrail Policies

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support explicit experiment safety, scope, stop-condition, and intervention policies distinct from backend-local operational safeguards.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Mature experiment systems need declared guardrails that shape what an experiment is permitted to do and when it must be constrained or stopped.
