# ASR-509 — Participant Replay, Causality, And History Verification

## Statement

The ecosystem shall provide verification surfaces for participant replay, causality, and history consistency where reproducibility or attribution is claimed.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Participant replay and attribution claims require explicit verification surfaces.
