# EXP-728 — Participant Episode Model

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support first-class participant episode records distinct from runs, trajectories, and derived evaluations.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that agent-supporting ecosystems often reason over bounded episodes as distinct experiment objects rather than only over full runs or isolated trajectories.
