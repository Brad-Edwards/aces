# GOV-926 — Federation-Level VV&A And Accreditation Basis

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support validation, verification, accreditation, and comparable assurance claims at the federation or composed-stack level, including assumptions, profiles, bridge dependencies, interoperability constraints, and evidence requirements beyond per-artifact conformance.

## Rationale

Federation-scale interoperability cannot be justified solely from artifact-local conformance. Primary-source military simulation practice highlights the need for federation-level VV&A and accreditation thinking that covers the composed system and its assumptions.
