# EXP-726 — Participant Benchmark Protocol Model

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support first-class benchmark protocols for participant-oriented tasks, including attempt structure, interaction limits, environment assumptions, and evaluation rules.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that participant-oriented benchmarks are not just scenarios; they depend on explicit protocol structure governing attempts, interaction, and evaluation.
