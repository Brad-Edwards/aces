# ASR-524 — Benchmark Truth And Adjudication Asset Integrity

## Statement

The ecosystem shall provide assurance mechanisms that verify declared benchmark truth assets, hidden evaluation assets, and adjudication assets remain properly separated, versioned, and traceable where such claims are made.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that benchmark integrity depends not only on tasks and trajectories but also on the trustworthy handling of truth and adjudication assets.
