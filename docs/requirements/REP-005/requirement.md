# REP-005 — Drive and validate CAGE-2 replication across the CybORG sim and the ACES reference backend

## Statement

ACES shall drive the single CAGE-2 SDL document (REP-003) through both the CybORG simulation-backend adapter (REP-004) and the ACES reference backend, and shall capture replication evidence demonstrating that the authored scenario is realized consistently across both backends against the equivalence criteria defined in REP-001. Evidence shall be reproducible and suitable as paper evidence. Scope explicitly excludes an emulation-backend realization of CAGE-2.

## Rationale

The demonstration itself: one scenario, two independent conformant backends, evidence of consistent realization. This is the portability-as-a-property-of-the-spec claim made concrete.
