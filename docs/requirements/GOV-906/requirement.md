# GOV-906 — Translation Loss And Ambiguity Disclosure

## Statement

The ecosystem shall disclose loss, ambiguity, unsupported meaning, and confidence boundaries when translating to or from adjacent ecosystems.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Translation must remain honest about what meaning is lost or changed.
