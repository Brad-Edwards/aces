# GOV-905 — Interoperable Exchange With Adjacent Ecosystems

## Statement

The ecosystem shall support interoperable exchange with adjacent scenario, workflow, CTI, and experiment ecosystems without collapsing native meaning.

## Rationale

Requirement inventory expansion. Mature ecosystems need exchange with adjacent ecosystems without losing their own conceptual integrity.
