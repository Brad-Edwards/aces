# ASR-520 — Participant Trajectory Corpus Conformance

## Statement

The ecosystem shall maintain conformance fixtures and checks for participant trajectory records, replay assets, and related interchange contracts.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that trajectory artifacts need assurance surfaces of their own rather than relying only on generic evidence or participant-contract validation.
