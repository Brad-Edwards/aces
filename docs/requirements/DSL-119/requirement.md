# DSL-119 — Participant Trace, Demonstration, And Replay References

## Statement

The language shall support references to participant trace, demonstration, replay, or reference-trajectory assets where scenarios or experiments require them.

## Rationale

Primary-source refresh shows that reference traces and demonstrations are important ecosystem assets for participant evaluation, replay, and learning, even when they are not part of every scenario.
