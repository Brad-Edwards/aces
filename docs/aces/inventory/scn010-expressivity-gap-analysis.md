# SCN-010 Expressivity Gap Analysis — Final Architect-Review Report
## RAES SDL runtime surface coverage for the 16 remaining APTL containers

## 1. Executive summary

The 16 remaining SCN-010 containers fall into seven functional groups — a search/index cluster tier (`wazuh.indexer`, `thehive-es`, `shuffle-opensearch`), an analytics dashboard (`wazuh.dashboard`), log-shipping/intel-sync sidecars (`wazuh-sidecar-db`, `wazuh-sidecar-suricata`, `misp-suricata-sync`) plus the standalone Suricata they govern, a threat-intelligence platform (`misp`), a Redis/MariaDB data-store-and-sync tier (`misp-redis`, `misp-db`), an IR/case-management platform over a wide-column store (`thehive`, `thehive-cassandra`), a SOAR tier (`shuffle-frontend`, `shuffle-orborus`, `shuffle-backend`), and an analyzer/responder engine (`cortex`). Holding each container to the **wazuh.manager parity bar** — wazuh.manager (a SIEM) earned a fully domain-typed `security_monitoring_managers` family rather than being folded into generic `filesystem`/`listeners`/`software` surfaces — surfaces a consistent failure mode: every one of these containers has *defining logical state* that RAES can only shallow-encode today (a Redis cache as a relational `RuntimeDatabaseService`, a search cluster as a relational engine, a MISP as content counts, a SOAR as a data volume, an embedded RBAC store as OS `/etc` identity). The cohesive response is **extension-first at the least structural cost that still clears parity**: collapse the recurring *shapes* into two discriminator-guarded spines plus three orthogonal shared primitives and one forwarder family, each spine **guarded by a `require_profile_for_<discriminator>` after-validator** so the abstraction provably cannot silently shallow-encode a defining fact, with inter-node access detail expressed as typed relationship subtypes, and a single executable cross-family invariant lint making "#439 one small invariant set" testable rather than aspirational.

Final proposed surfaces:

- **`runtime.datastore_services`** (new family) — one datastore spine, `data_model` discriminator (`search_index | wide_column | key_value | relational`, **OPEN** per the adversarial verdict), guarded by `require_profile_for_data_model`. Covers OpenSearch/ES, Cassandra, Redis.
- **`runtime.platform_applications`** (new family) — one platform spine, `platform_kind` discriminator (`analytics_dashboard | threat_intel | case_management | analyzer_engine | soar`), guarded by `require_profile_for_platform_kind`. Covers the dashboard, MISP, TheHive, Cortex, Shuffle.
- **`runtime.app_authorizations`** (shared primitive) — application-internal RBAC typed **once** (highest-recurrence gap, 8+ sites), `resource_vocabulary` discriminator, tier derived from the owning reference.
- **`runtime.scheduled_jobs`** (shared primitive) — cadence + run-state **only** (inputs/outputs/trigger_targets removed per verdict to fence off the forwarder double-encoding).
- **`runtime.forwarding_agents`** (new family) — agent-side `(source, transform, ship-target, buffer)` shipping state; `agent_kind` discriminator (reclassified OPEN per verdict); covers both log-forwarders and the intel-sync co-process.
- **`runtime.orchestration_authorities`** (shared primitive) — container-spawn control authority, referencing the existing `RuntimeControlInterface` shell.
- **Relationship subtypes** `RelationshipForwardingEdge`, `RelationshipServiceIntegration`, `RelationshipProxyUpstream` + a `RuntimeApplicationRoute.upstream_target` field extension.
- **Named confirmation-folds** (no new structure) — Suricata, `misp-db` relational, OS-local identity, transport tier — proving the parity gate cuts both ways.

## 2. Method & depth bar

### 2.1 The parity contract

SCN-010 requires **full observable parity**: every fact a participant/adversary/defender/agent/evaluator could observe from inside the realized range at steady state must be either encoded in `techvault.sdl.yaml` using RAES *or* blocked by a specific RAES expressivity issue. "Captured in evidence", "representative subset", "load-bearing fields only", "APTL can't consume it yet", and "out of scope" are forbidden completion claims. Therefore: **any observable fact RAES cannot *type* today is an expressivity gap.**

### 2.2 Why wazuh.manager sets the depth standard

The reference exemplar is `aptl/docs/aces/inventory/wazuh.manager/` (README + mapping-ledger). wazuh.manager — a SIEM — was *not* folded into generic `filesystem_inventory`/`service_listeners`/`runtime_software`. It earned an entire typed family, `security_monitoring_managers`, capturing its defining logical state: manager/agent/component topology, content corpora (`content_sets`), `detection_definitions` (6,121 of them, as a *bounded parsed manifest* — not raw rule-body import), and provenance-bearing settings. The depth bar is therefore: **a container's *defining* logical state must be domain-typed to the same standard, not absorbed into a generic surface.** A Redis cache modeled as a relational `RuntimeDatabaseService`, a MISP modeled as `service_listeners` + content counts, or a SOAR modeled as a `/shuffle-database` filesystem volume is the exact shallow encoding the parity gate forbids.

### 2.3 Derivation and adversarial filtering

Gaps were derived per-container by enumerating defining logical state (the `defining_state` corpus), testing each fact against the *closest* existing runtime surface field-by-field, and recording inadequacy only where the existing surface's **shape** cannot carry the fact (not merely "no instance authored yet"). Every candidate was then put through an adversarial challenge rubric of six falsifiable questions: *is it a real gap; is it expressible today (cite the exact field that would carry it); is it a local optimization; is the grounding primary-literature-sufficient; does it meet parity depth; and what required revisions or rejections follow.* The verdicts are incorporated below: where the rubric returned `confirm`, the surface stands; where `revise`, the required revisions are applied (e.g. `data_model` and `agent_kind` reclassified OPEN; `scheduled_jobs` hollowed to cadence-only; the `event` schedule member dropped; `owner_kind` collapsed into a derived tier); where a candidate would have been expressible today, it is recorded as a **rejected candidate** in §4.6 to strengthen credibility.

**Review model and its limits.** The "adversarial challenge" above is an
architect-guided adversarial self-review, not an external review panel. A single
author (the architect) applied the fixed six-question rubric to each candidate
gap and recorded a `confirm`, `revise`, or `reject` verdict; `revise` verdicts
were reconciled by applying the stated revision inline, and `reject` verdicts are
listed as rejected candidates in §4.6. There were no independent external
reviewers — "rubric" and "verdict" name the self-applied questions and their
outcomes, not the judgement of a separate body. This report is therefore
architect self-review held to a peer-review-grade standard; it has not been
independently peer reviewed.

All load-bearing code claims in this report were re-verified against the live tree:
- `DatabaseObjectType` is closed to `database | schema | table` (`runtime_database_vocab.py:89–94` — even narrower than the gap text's "view" claim).
- `DatabaseEngine.REDIS`/`DatabaseProtocol.REDIS` exist as enum members but `DatabaseEngine.MARIADB → DatabaseProtocol.MYSQL` is the only relational mapping; there is no CQL/OpenSearch pairing (`runtime_database_vocab.py:122–126`).
- `RuntimeControlInterface` (`runtime_mounts.py:170`) has **no id field** and is held anonymously on `RuntimeConfiguration.local_control_interfaces` (`runtime_configuration.py:357`).
- `RuntimeApplicationRoute` (`runtime_application.py:263`) has no upstream/origin field.
- `RuntimeIdentityRelationshipKind` = `member_of | trusts | federates_with` only (`runtime_directory_identity.py:98–104`); `RuntimeIdentityPolicyKind` is a closed enum.
- `SshServerConfig` (`runtime_ssh_server.py:303`) violates the `Runtime*` class-name rule; `runtime_configuration.py:358–359` carries the `process`-scalar + `processes`-list twin; `runtime_mail_service.py:111–128` uses module-level free-function validators (the `#442` outlier).
- The registry shape is `RuntimeServiceFamily(collection_name, id_field, child_refs)` (`_runtime_service_families.py:33–40`).

## 4. Gap inventory

Each confirmed/revised gap below states what cannot be typed, why the closest existing surface is insufficient (with exact fields), the parity-depth justification, and primary-literature anchors. Verdict-mandated revisions are folded in.

### 4.1 Datastore tier (OSC-01…09, MISP-DSS-01, GAP-WIDECOLUMN-DATASTORE)

**What cannot be typed.** Cluster identity + node-role membership + master election (`_cat/nodes` `dimr`, `node.role 'cdfhilmrstw' master '*'`); indices with shard/replica geometry, health, time-rolling patterns (`pri=3 rep=0`, `wazuh-alerts-4.x-2026.05.30`); composable templates + write-index aliases (`composed_of`, `.kibana → .kibana_1`); field mappings (`index.mapping.total_fields.limit=10000`); ISM/ILM lifecycle phases; ordered ingest pipelines; engine-internal plugins (18 @ 2.19.1.0); scoped settings + intra-cluster transport mTLS + snapshot repositories (`_snapshot {}` — observable absence); Cassandra keyspaces with `SimpleStrategy/NetworkTopologyStrategy`, replication factor, per-DC factor map, ring/partitioner/token ownership; Redis 16 numbered logical DBs, keyspace datatype census, RDB save-points `3600 1 300 100 60 10000` / AOF off, `maxmemory-policy=noeviction`, pub/sub channels, streams.

**Why existing surfaces are insufficient.** `runtime.database_services` is irreducibly relational: `DatabaseObjectType` is **closed to `database | schema | table`** (`runtime_database_vocab.py:89–94`), the mandatory `Database → DatabaseSchema → DatabaseTable` nesting has no analogue for a keyspace (no schema layer), a sharded/replicated/time-rolling index, or a numbered Redis logical DB; `DatabaseGrant` targets only those three object types — never `index_pattern`/`key_pattern`/`keyspace`/`command_category`; there is no field for `replication_factor`, shard/replica geometry, node roles, persistence/eviction, ring topology, or role-mappings. `DatabaseEngine.REDIS` exists as a member but resolves to a relational body. `security_monitoring_managers` is the wrong domain (the indexer is the storage tier *behind* Wazuh, not the manager).

**Parity-depth justification.** Indices/shards are to a search cluster what databases/tables are to the relational family — the primary first-class objects — and replication strategy + factor *are* the engineering essence of a wide-column store. Folding any non-relational store into the relational family silently drops the defining availability fact (RF), the node-role topology, or the persistence/eviction posture — exactly the shallow encoding the parity gate forbids.

**Primary-literature anchors.** Codd, *A Relational Model of Data*, CACM 13(6) 1970 and ISO/IEC 9075 SQL (delimit the relational boundary precisely, so non-relational shapes are *provably* outside it); Zobel & Moffat, *Inverted Files for Text Search Engines*, ACM CSUR 38(2) 2006 and Ongaro & Ousterhout (Raft), USENIX ATC 2014 (inverted-index shard model, elected-coordinator topology); Lakshman & Malik, *Cassandra*, ACM SIGOPS OSR 44(2) 2010, DeCandia et al., *Dynamo*, SOSP 2007, Chang et al., *Bigtable*, OSDI 2006, Gilbert & Lynch 2002 (CAP) (wide-column RF/consistency); Redis RESP3/ACL/persistence/eviction references and OASIS AMQP 1.0 (ISO/IEC 19464:2014) (key-value/broker primitives); NIST SP 800-92, SP 800-53 AU-11/CP-9, SP 800-209 (retention/snapshot/storage hardening); RFC 8446 + RFC 5280 (intra-cluster mTLS).

### 4.2 Platform-application tier (ADASH-1…4, TIP-001…004, GAP-IR-CASEMGMT-PLATFORM, CORTEX-001…004, SOAR-WORKFLOW-PLATFORM)

**What cannot be typed.** The dashboard's `.kibana` saved-object graph (typed kinds: index-pattern/visualization/dashboard/search/config, stable ids, **typed inter-object references** visualization→index-pattern); the MISP TI corpora as *structured* vocabularies (feeds with provider/url/`source_format`/`input_source`/distribution; 165 taxonomies with namespace/exclusive trees; 112 galaxies with `GalaxyCluster` knowledge graphs and typed `uses/similar/subtechnique-of` relations; 122 warninglists with `match_type=string|substring|hostname|cidr|regex`; object-template attribute schemas); the 5-level distribution lattice + sharing-groups; orgs/tenants; SOAR workflows/app-catalog/triggers/environments; Cortex analyzer/responder catalog (datatype scope, docker-vs-process runner, kind) + job-execution model + rate-limits; outbound upstream data-source bindings (`wazuh.yml` API host `1513629884013 → https://wazuh.manager:55000`).

**Why existing surfaces are insufficient.** `runtime.applications` types only inbound HTTP routes the app *serves* — not its content graph, tenants, or upstream bindings, and the dashboard saved objects live in the `.kibana` OpenSearch index, not on the node filesystem. `identity_authorities` models a *wire-protocol directory authority* (LDAP/AD/SAML/OIDC/SCIM) with closed `RuntimeIdentityRelationshipKind` = `member_of | trusts | federates_with` (`runtime_directory_identity.py:98–104`) — it cannot express a feed/taxonomy/galaxy/saved-object content graph. `security_monitoring_managers.content_sets` is one opaque `THREAT_INTEL`/rule-corpus kind carrying `kind/format/file_count/loaded` — it cannot carry a taxonomy namespace tree, a galaxy-cluster graph with typed edges, a warninglist match-type, a saved-object reference graph, or an analyzer datatype/runner catalog. Folding a TI/SOAR/case/dashboard platform there is the category error (dissemination/automation platform ≠ SIEM) the cohesion mandate warns against.

**Parity-depth justification.** wazuh.manager earned typed `content_sets` + `detection_definitions` for its *defining* corpus instead of filesystem dumps. The dashboard saved-object graph, the four MISP TI vocabularies, the SOAR workflow graph, and the Cortex analyzer/responder catalog are the equivalent defining corpora and warrant equal typed-graph depth — encoded as a **bounded parsed manifest** (parse the object graph + typed references + marking refs + evidence refs; never raw-body import), mirroring the detection-definition precedent. The `require_profile_for_platform_kind` guard (§5.2) makes the empty-MISP failure mode (the misp ledger's documented force-fit of orgs into `identity_authorities` and TI corpora "not exercised") impossible: `threat_intel` MUST carry ≥1 taxonomy with namespace, ≥1 galaxy_cluster with typed relations, ≥1 warninglist with match_type, ≥1 feed with provider/source_format, ≥1 sharing_group — a generic empty object list fails validation.

**Primary-literature anchors.** W3C RDF 1.1 and Angles & Gutierrez, *Survey of Graph Database Models*, ACM CSUR 2008 (typed inter-object reference graph); OASIS STIX 2.1 + TAXII 2.1, MISP core data model (taxonomies/galaxies/warninglists/feeds/sharing-groups), FIRST TLP 2.0, MITRE ATT&CK, NIST SP 800-150, ISO/IEC 27010 (TI corpora + releasability lattice); NIST SP 800-61r2/r3 (case management + analyzer/responder course-of-action); OASIS CACAO v2.0 + OpenC2 (SOAR workflow/action vocabulary — reused for workflow *graph structure* while this surface adds the node-scoped *runtime inventory* CACAO does not provide); NIST SP 800-92 + ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 (presentation-over-store tier, typed component bindings); RFC 9110 + RFC 7239 (inbound-route vs outbound-upstream); RFC 6749 (API-key bearer principals).

### 4.3 Application-internal RBAC (OSC-07, ADASH-2/3, CORTEX-002, + RBAC facets of TIP/MISP/Cassandra/Redis/TheHive)

**What cannot be typed.** Application-internal principals in the plugin/app's own user DB (OpenSearch `internal_users` admin/kibanaserver/logstash with bcrypt hashes + `backend_roles` + reserved/hidden flags; Cassandra `system_auth.roles` with superuser; Redis ACL `user default on ~* &* +@all`; MISP orgs/users/roles/api-keys; Cortex/TheHive org-users + api-key principals + profiles), roles with **index-pattern / CQL-resource / key-pattern / app-resource permission sets**, role-mappings keyed on backend-roles/users/hosts/DNs, dashboard tenants, and the auth-enabled/disabled posture.

**Why existing surfaces are insufficient.** `runtime.identity` is OS `/etc` passwd/group/sudo — these are not OS users. `runtime.identity_authorities` has `RuntimeIdentityRelationship` carrying only `member_of/trusts/federates_with` and a closed `RuntimeIdentityPolicyKind` — **no resource-scoped `permission_grant` (role → action → resource_pattern)** exists, and it presupposes a wire protocol these embedded stores lack. `DatabaseGrant.object_type` is the closed `database | schema | table` enum that cannot express `index_pattern`/`cql_resource`/`redis_acl`/`app_resource` scopes. None fit.

**Parity-depth justification.** This is the single **highest-recurrence gap in the entire inventory (8+ sites)** and a complete RBAC authorization subsystem — the most security-relevant logical state of each store/platform. Typing it per-app would produce ~8 divergent one-off RBAC models (the textbook `#439` fork). It is **orthogonal to both** the datastore shape and the platform shape, so it is extracted once as a referenced primitive.

**Primary-literature anchors.** Ferraiolo & Kuhn RBAC (NIST 1992); Sandhu et al. RBAC96 (IEEE Computer 29(2) 1996); ANSI INCITS 359-2004 (users→roles→permissions, role hierarchy, SoD — the **resource-scoped permission_grant is the defining delta** `identity_authorities` deliberately omits); NIST SP 800-162 ABAC + SP 800-178 (resource-scoping + attribute role-mapping); Saltzer & Schroeder, Proc. IEEE 63(9) 1975 (subject/object protection split); Bell & LaPadula MTR-2997 1976, Sandhu *Lattice-Based Access Control*, IEEE Computer 26(11) 1993, NIST SP 800-145 (tenant isolation); RFC 5280 (admin_dn/nodes_dn cert principals); RFC 6749/7617 (API-key vs password principal).

### 4.4 Forwarder / intel-sync agents (GAP-SIDECAR-FORWARDER, GAP-INTEL-SYNC-PIPELINE)

**What cannot be typed.** The agent-side `(source, transform, ship-target, buffer)` tuple: tailed-path + `parse_format` (`/logs/eve.json` as json, `/logs/pg_log/...` as syslog), ship target (`wazuh.manager:1514` event / `:1515` enrollment), `client_buffer` (queue 5000, eps 500, crypto aes, time-reconnect 60); and the intel-sync pipeline (MISP pull + tag selector `aptl:enforce`, IOC→rule transform with `SID_BASE=99000000`, output `misp-iocs.rules`, Suricata socket reload).

**Why existing surfaces are insufficient.** `RuntimeSecurityMonitoringManager.agents` (`runtime_security_monitoring.py:504–505`) is a manager-side enrolled-agent *roster* (agent_id/name/status/address/node_ref/group_refs) with **no** `(source,transform,ship-target,buffer)` tuple. `RuntimeNetworkDetectionRuleSource.generated_by` (`runtime_network_detection.py:215`) records the *consumed* source, not the *producer* co-process. `filesystem`/`processes`/`service_units` can only attest a file/process exists, and importing `ossec.conf`/`filebeat.yml` verbatim violates the no-vendor-config-import doctrine.

**Parity-depth justification.** wazuh.manager earned a full family for the *manager* half of the agent/manager relationship; the forwarder is the structurally co-equal *agent* half and the single most-replicated steady-state role in the lab. The intel-sync co-process is the **same** `source→transform→target` shape in the intel-to-content direction, so it folds here as `agent_kind=content_sync` rather than a third family — the recurs-across-groups analysis explicitly asked whether one generalized forwarder subsumes both, and it does.

**Primary-literature anchors.** RFC 5424 (Syslog), RFC 5425 (Syslog over TLS), RFC 6587 (Syslog over TCP), RFC 3164 (BSD syslog parse) (`(source,format,transport)`); NIST SP 800-92 (source→collector→aggregator tier); NIST SP 800-137 (ISCM — forwarding posture as monitored state); STIX 2.1 / TAXII 2.1 + NIST SP 800-150 + Bianco (Pyramid of Pain) + MITRE ATT&CK (intel pull-source/transform → detection); Elastic Beats/Filebeat harvester and OpenTelemetry Logs receiver→processor→exporter (implementation lineage, *not* schema authority).

### 4.5 Orchestration authority, scheduled jobs, and typed edges

**Orchestration authority (SOAR-ORCH-AUTHORITY, CORTEX-005).** `RuntimeControlInterface` (`runtime_mounts.py:170`) types the docker.sock as a present read-write unix socket — the **shell** — but carries no field for what the holder is authorized to *do* (spawn image X into scope Y under policy Z). The spawn contract (`SHUFFLE_WORKER_IMAGE`, API 1.44, org/env scope, lifecycle, 550 realized children, host-root-equivalent privilege) is untypeable, and `RuntimeControlInterface` has **no id field** to point at — a hard ref-blocker the reconciliation epic must fix. Anchors: OCI Runtime/Image Specs; Kubernetes controller pattern; NIST SP 800-190 (orchestrator trust boundary); CIS Docker Benchmark 5.x; MITRE ATT&CK T1610/T1611; Docker Engine API v1.44 (lineage).

**Scheduled jobs (MISP-DSS-02, TIP-004).** `service_units.ServiceUnitKind.TIMER` carries only systemd lifecycle (load/active/enabled/exit_code/main_pid) and is structurally systemd-scoped, so a bare-container ENTRYPOINT loop cannot be encoded. Per the adversarial verdict this primitive is **hollowed to cadence + run-state only** — `inputs/outputs/trigger_targets` are removed (they belong to the referencing `forwarding_agent`, fencing off the double-encoding hazard), the `event` schedule member is dropped (event-triggered work is a trigger relationship, not a recurrence), and the Resque `queue_role` facet is dropped as vendor-config-shaped (MISP worker pools are modeled as `processes` + per-worker `scheduled_jobs`). Anchors: POSIX.1-2017 crontab; RFC 5545 (iCalendar RRULE); systemd.timer + Kubernetes CronJob (lineage); NIST SP 800-92/800-137 (run-state observability).

**Typed edges (GAP-FORWARDER-MANAGER-EDGE, CORTEX-006, PROXY-UPSTREAM-EDGE, ADASH-4).** A generic `Relationship.properties: dict[str,str]` can assert a link but cannot structurally validate enrollment-identity classification, enum sentinels, or cross-ref resolution — the same reason `RelationshipDatabaseAccess`/`RelationshipMailAccess` earned subtypes (cross-ref resolution lives in `validator.py:_verify_relationship_database_access`, not in-class). Three subtypes carry the domain access detail; `target_listener_role` **reuses** the existing `RuntimeSecurityMonitoringListenerRole` enum (`runtime_security_monitoring.py:100–105`) rather than forking a parallel vocab. Anchors: OASIS STIX 2.1 SRO; RFC 5424/5425/6587/3164; NIST SP 800-92 §4; RFC 6749; RFC 9110 + RFC 7239; NIST SP 800-44; Kubernetes Ingress/Gateway API.

### 4.6 Rejected candidate-gaps (expressible today — confirmation-folds)

Recording these strengthens the analysis's credibility by proving the parity lens cuts **against over-building** too:

- **Suricata standalone IDS** — *expressible today* via `network_detection_engines` (`RuntimeNetworkDetectionRuleSource.generated_by` + `THREAT_INTEL` kind at `runtime_network_detection.py:89,215`, output_streams `eve_json`, control_channels `rule_reload`, network_sets) + `network_sensors` (capture posture). Its defining state *is* detection-engine state, typed field-for-field. **Not a gap.**
- **`misp-db`** — *expressible today* via `database_services`: `DatabaseEngine.MARIADB → DatabaseProtocol.MYSQL` (`runtime_database_vocab.py:28,123`), Database/Schema/Table/Role/Grant/Settings tree fits a genuine relational engine (the exact opposite of the Redis-as-relational mismatch). **Not a gap** — relational→datastore fold deferred as named coherence debt.
- **OS-local `/etc` users/groups/sudo** — *expressible today* via `runtime.identity` (`RuntimeLocalIdentityInventory`, `runtime_identity.py:193`). The MISP/TheHive *app-internal* RBAC is **not** this — it routes to `app_authorization`.
- **Transport tier** of every container (TLS listeners, HTTP routes, env/TLS material) — *expressible today* via `service_listeners` + `applications` + `runtime_configuration`. The gap is everything *above* transport.

These are shipped as a first-class "deliberate confirmation-folds / non-gaps" artifact (a new subsection in `limitations.md` per the verdict), each with field-for-field justification, so a reviewer cannot claim either under-coverage or over-building. Critically, the MISP fold is **conditional**: it discharges only MISP's OS-identity + transport slices; MISP's defining state is discharged by `platform_applications` + `app_authorization`, so the fold is valid *only if* those siblings land.

## 5. Cohesive architecture

**Governing decision rule (applied uniformly):** same defining-fact **shape** → one family with an open discriminator + required per-class profile; different shape → peer family; recurring fact orthogonal to every owning family → shared primitive referenced (not re-typed); inter-node access detail → relationship subtype; existing family whose shape already matches → named fold (proven, not assumed).

## 7. Cross-SDL coherence statement

After this work the entire runtime surface (old + new) is describable by **one small invariant set**, enforced as an executable lint over every registered family:

1. **Identity.** Every observed instance carries `<noun>_id`, `require_symbol`-validated (concrete, no `${var}`); native engine/product ids are observed *data*, never reference keys.
2. **Class & module.** Every public model is `Runtime<Noun>`; every family lives in `runtime_<family>.py` (+ `_vocab` sibling under the 600-line cap).
3. **Containers plural.** Every list of typed children is a plural field.
4. **Enum sentinels.** Open taxonomy → `unknown` + `other`; closed standard-fixed vocab → neither.
5. **Validation one way.** In-class `@model_validator(after)` `validate_<noun>` calling private `_reject_duplicate_*`; no free functions; `parse_runtime_enum_or_var` the only enum helper.
6. **Required-profile guard.** Every discriminated spine fails validation on an under-populated instance.
7. **Secrets.** One shared redaction helper; secret-named settings/principals/inputs omit raw values and classify `redacted`/`operator_secret`.
8. **Registry.** Every family registers through `_runtime_service_families.py` with `collection_name`/`id_field`/`child_refs`; export-name uniqueness holds.
9. **Decomposition.** Same fact-shape → one guarded family; different shape → peer family; orthogonal recurring fact → referenced shared primitive; inter-node detail → relationship subtype; matching existing shape → named fold.

This is built to survive expert review because the cohesion is **structural and executable, not aspirational**: ~10 candidate one-off families collapse to 2 guarded spines + 3 primitives + 1 forwarder family + 3 relationship subtypes; the highest-recurrence gap (app-internal RBAC, 8+ sites) is typed once; the `require_profile_for_<discriminator>` guards make over-abstraction *fail* (Redis-as-relational, MISP-as-counts, SOAR-as-object-list cannot pass); the named folds prove the gate is not over-applied; and the `#442` narrative is corrected to the verifiable codebase reality. The lint makes that cohesion claim testable rather than asserted.

## 9. Residual risks & open questions

1. **Datastore-spine breadth.** One spine spanning search/wide-column/key-value carries engine-specific optional child fields. The `require_profile_for_data_model` guard + open `engine`/`data_model` enums keep instances shaped, but reviewers must keep the per-data_model required-profile table current; document/graph stores may warrant a peer family in a future epic if their optional-field surface grows unwieldy. (`document` is dropped now — no observed realizer.)
2. **Platform-spine breadth.** Leans on an OPEN `content_object.kind`.
